Trim privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Trim is made by Hafsoh Bashar

The short version

Trim reads the prompt you are typing on claude.ai so it can suggest a shorter version. It does this inside your browser. Your prompts are never sent to me or to anyone else, and Trim has no server.

What Trim reads

Only the text in the message box on claude.ai, and only while the page is open. Trim does not read your past conversations, Claude's replies, or any other website.

What Trim stores

  • Whether Trim is switched on or off.
  • A running total of roughly how many tokens you have saved.

Both are kept in your browser's extension storage on your own computer. They contain no prompt text. Removing Trim deletes them.

What Trim does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not make network requests.
  • It only changes your prompt when you choose "Use this", and you can undo it.
