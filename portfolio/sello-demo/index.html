Sello · main feature

Fix a reply in two taps

Sello answers your Instagram and TikTok DMs. When it gets something wrong, you see exactly where the answer came from, fix the source, and Sello tells the customer for you.

  1. Open Chioma's chat and tap Why? under Sello's first reply.
  2. Tap Fix this, pick what's wrong, then say what's right.
  3. Watch Sello correct itself. Hit Undo if you change your mind.
  4. Tap the grey banner to check her payment and confirm the order.
