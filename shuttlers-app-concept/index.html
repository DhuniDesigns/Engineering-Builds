Concept by Hafsoh Bashar

The ride, from booking to boarding.

Four screens for the Shuttlers app, built on the parts riders already know, with fixes for what they complain about most: a map that freezes, buses that leave without a warning, pickup points that are hard to find and money stuck in a wallet. Every screen works, so tap through it.
