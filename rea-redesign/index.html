    Who's involved

    For communities · REUCS

    Power that stays on, together.

    A Rural Electricity Users Cooperative Society (REUCS) gives a benefiting community a shared voice in its electricity project. REA runs community engagement to help communities form one.

    Ask about forming a REUCS →
    1. Learn to run itMembers are trained to own, operate, maintain and stay safe around their electricity facilities.
    2. Use power productivelyGuidance on productive use, saving energy and using it efficiently.
    3. Protect the equipmentThe community works together to guard against theft and vandalism.
    4. Keep it affordableREUCS partners with DisCos and power producers on fair tariffs and encourages prompt payment.
    5. Solve problems togetherA forum where stakeholders resolve the community's electricity issues.

    FAQs

    Answers before you apply.

    Need help with an application?

    Talk to the REA team by phone, email or at our Abuja office.

    Call +234 811 249 4040
    How can I benefit from REA programmes?+

    Individuals, communities, businesses and developers can benefit by applying through a programme's portal or by taking part in electrification initiatives in their area.

    How can my community get electricity through REA?+

    Communities can submit a request through REA's official channels, or be selected for an ongoing programme based on need, feasibility and impact.

    Are REA electricity services free?+

    Some projects are fully funded by government or donors. Others involve cost-sharing or private sector participation, depending on the programme.

    How long does it take to electrify a community?+

    It depends on the project's size, location and funding — anywhere from a few months to over a year.

    How can I become a developer under REA programmes?+

    Apply through programme-specific portals such as NEP or REF and meet that programme's eligibility criteria, generally technical capacity, financial capability and regulatory compliance.

    What is a REUCS?+

    A Rural Electricity Users Cooperative Society. REA mobilises benefiting communities to form one, so members are trained to use, protect and pay for their electricity, and the project keeps running long after commissioning.
