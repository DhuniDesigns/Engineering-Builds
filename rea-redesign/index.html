    FAQs

    Answers before you apply.

    Need help with an application?

    Talk to the REA team by phone, email or at our Abuja office.

    Call +234 811 249 4040
    How can my community get electricity from REA?+

    Check the eligibility requirements for the programme that fits your community, then apply when a window is open. REA also identifies communities through its national mapping exercise, which has covered more than 150,000 unserved or underserved communities.

    I'm a developer. How do I access grants?+

    Programmes such as DARES and the Africa Mini-grids Programme support private developers to build mini-grids and deploy solar systems. Calls for proposals and tenders are published with deadlines and eligibility criteria.

    How do I know when a programme opens?+

    Programmes open in funding windows. Subscribe to updates or follow REA's official channels to hear when the next call for applications is published.

    What is a REUCS?+

    A Rural Electricity Users Cooperative Society. Benefiting communities form one so they share ownership of their electrification project and keep it running long after commissioning.

    How do I pay REA fees online?+

    All payments to REA go through the official Remita biller page, linked in the bar at the top of every page.
